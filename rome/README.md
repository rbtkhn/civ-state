# Rome

This lane is intentionally reserved and explicitly deferred.

Rome is the final fill lane for this corpus. It is not yet an active authored lane, and it should not be mistaken for a mature surface beside America, Russia, China, or Persia.

## What This Lane Will Eventually Hold

When it is built, `Rome` will mean the Roman civilizational succession problem in the large sense:

- classical Rome
- imperial Rome
- Byzantium as a major Roman continuation
- Latin Christendom
- later Roman-successor legitimacy struggles
- the modern West as a partial successor, not as a simple identity with Rome

## What It Will Not Mean

This lane is not reserved merely for:

- ancient Rome as an antiquarian topic
- modern Italy as a state proxy
- Byzantium as a separate sibling lane

Byzantium belongs inside the Rome lane as one of its major transformed continuities.

## Why It Is Deferred

Rome is being filled last because it is one of the largest succession-civilization carriers in the whole corpus. It will need to hold internal tensions that are too large to fake with a thin early scaffold.
