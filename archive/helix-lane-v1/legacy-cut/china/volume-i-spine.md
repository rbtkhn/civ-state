# China Volume I Spine

WORK only; not Record.

This note is the design contract for **Volume I: China** in the five-volume `civ-emp` series. It fixes the first lane-volume at twenty chapters grouped into four parts of five chapters each. The spine is not generic. It is meant to carry the specific China problem: how a civilization built around order, continuity, and administrative carry can widen its influence without inheriting visible imperial policing, and how that same civilizational strength can harden into blindness, rigidity, or over-managed calm.

Each row below names the working chapter title, chapter type, chapter purpose, likely governing destination-corpus surfaces, and the unique work the chapter must do in the volume.
