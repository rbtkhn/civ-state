## Transaction Name

Anti-disorder nuclear restraint compact.

## State Lane

## Source Inputs

- Scoreboards used: Pape coercive-center index; Mearsheimer structural-realist scoreboard.

## Civilization / Empire Helix Input

- Civilization input: Chinese legitimacy centers order, continuity, competence, and prevention of chaos.
- Empire input: China wants secure energy, trade stability, sanctions workarounds, and reduced U.S. coercive reach.
- Statecraft regulation: Preserve Iran as counterpressure without enabling a nuclear shock that destabilizes energy and regional order.
- Relevant lane helix: China helix.

## State Interest

China's durable interest is to prevent war and proliferation chaos while keeping Iran resilient enough to reduce unilateral U.S. coercive power.

## Leverage / Constraint

- Leverage being spent: Energy purchases, finance, diplomatic convening, technical cooperation, sanctions workarounds, and quiet pressure.
- Constraint that limits the leverage: China cannot let nuclear escalation or preventive war damage energy flows, insurance, shipping, or industrial stability.
- Volatility note: A threshold Iran can help multipolarity; a breakout crisis can harm China's core anti-disorder interest.

## Richelieu Validation

- Institutional carrier: Foreign ministry, energy firms, state banks, technical agencies, and mediator forums.
- Capacity: Payment channels, investment sequencing, diplomatic pressure, and verification support.
- Continuity risk: Public ownership of a nuclear bargain may make China responsible for violations.
- Long-pressure burden: China must keep support quiet enough to preserve optionality.

## Bismarck Validation

- Limited aim: Freeze weaponization pressure and reduce war risk.
- Actor to isolate: Preventive-war actors and breakout advocates.
- Actor to reassure, restrain, or keep neutral: Iran, Gulf states, Russia, India, and Europe.
- Alliance geometry: Keep Iran resilient but not unconstrained; keep Russia aligned without letting Moscow dominate the frame.
- Restraint after advantage: Convert leverage into regional architecture rather than public victory.

## Instrument Text

Draft clause:

> China shall support a regional nuclear-restraint compact that recognizes Iran's civilian nuclear rights under enhanced verification, pairs non-weaponization commitments with phased economic relief, and creates a standing consultation channel for China, Russia, Gulf states, and other affected importers when threats of attack or breakout raise regional energy risk.

## Mirror Test

- America: This reduces U.S. control over the nuclear frame.
- Russia: This may be too stabilizing before Washington pays full strategic cost.
- China: This must not make Beijing guarantor of Iranian compliance.
- Iran: This may restrain Iran more than it recognizes Iran.

## Falsifiers / Revisit Triggers

- Falsifier: China refuses to link nuclear restraint to economic relief or regional architecture.
- Revisit trigger: Energy shock, IAEA access shift, China/Russia joint proposal, or Gulf request for Chinese mediation.
- Scoreboard movement: Review if Pape leverage rises but energy disorder begins damaging China directly.
- Actor signal: Chinese proposal, payment-channel guarantee, quiet pressure on Tehran, or importer coalition statement.
