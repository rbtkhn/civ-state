# Founding Provenance

This repo is a curated founding cut from a larger workshop environment.

## What Moved

The founding export keeps:

- the four active helix-first lanes: America, Russia, China, and Persia
- the active synthesis layer
- the active orientation and clause-review layer
- selected transactions, sheets, and source indexes that materially support retrieval and drafting
- one Persia proof object

## What Stayed Upstream

The founding export deliberately leaves behind most workshop-only control surfaces, including:

- transfer probes
- pilot-lane audits
- verification archaeology
- ledgers, manifests, and fine-grained migration accounting
- broader speaker-workshop intake and audit residue

Those materials still matter as workshop history, but they are not the primary authored corpus.

## Persia Naming Rule

This repo uses **Persia** as the lane-facing civilizational name for the material founded from the upstream Iran lane.

That choice is intentional:

- it signals a longer civilizational succession than the present state alone
- it does not deny the importance of modern Iranian carriers
- it does not erase upstream provenance

The upstream workshop may continue to use `iran/` operational paths until a separate rename is chosen there.
